Title: Senior AI Engineer - Agentic Systems & RAG
Company: ApexAI Solutions
Location: Remote / New York, NY

Overview:
ApexAI Solutions is seeking a Senior AI Engineer to design and implement production-grade agentic workflows, scalable Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems, and enterprise LLM integration pipelines. In this role, you will lead the architecture of stateful AI workflows and build high-performance Python services.

- LLM System Optimization: Implement structured JSON generation, optimize prompt context budgets, and implement caching layers for cost and rate-limit management.

Must-Have Qualifications:
- 3+ years of professional backend software development experience in Python (FastAPI, AsyncIO, Pydantic).
- Demonstrated experience building multi-agent systems with LangGraph (state management, conditional branching, checkpointers).
- Hands-on experience with vector databases, dense embeddings, and hybrid retrieval techniques.
- Solid background in containerization (Docker) and modern CI/CD deployment pipelines.

Nice-to-Haves:
- Experience integrating Microsoft Copilot Studio, Power Platform connectors, or Azure OpenAI services.
